
CODEBOOK: Health and Lifestyle Study

-----------------------------------------
1. Main Dataset: health_lifestyle_study_main.csv
-----------------------------------------

Variable                  | Description
--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------
ParticipantID             | Unique identifier for each participant (1–1500)
Region                    | Geographic region of residence (Urban, Suburban, Rural)
Age                       | Age of participant in years
Weight_Before             | Weight at baseline in pounds
Exercise_Min_per_Week     | Minutes of exercise per week
Smoker                    | Whether participant is a smoker (Yes/No)
Takes_Medication          | Whether participant takes medication (Yes/No)
Hypertension              | Hypertension status at baseline (Yes/No)
Diet_Type                 | Primary dietary pattern (Omnivore, Vegetarian, Vegan, Keto)
Education                 | Highest level of education (High School, Some College, Bachelor, Graduate)
Blood_Pressure_Before     | Systolic blood pressure at baseline in mmHg

-----------------------------------------
2. Paired Dataset: health_lifestyle_study_paired.csv
-----------------------------------------

Includes all columns from the main dataset plus:

Weight_After              | Weight after 12-week exercise program in pounds
Blood_Pressure_After      | Systolic blood pressure after program in mmHg
Hypertension_After        | Hypertension classification after program (Yes/No)

-----------------------------------------
3. Matched Pairs Dataset: health_lifestyle_study_matched.csv
-----------------------------------------

MatchID                   | ID for matched pair (1001–1060)
Diet_Assignment           | Assigned diet type (High-Fiber, Low-Carb)
Age                       | Age of participant in years
BMI                       | Body Mass Index (kg/m²)
Cholesterol               | Blood cholesterol after 8-week diet in mg/dL
